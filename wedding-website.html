Locale Congregation of De Castro, Metro Manila East

The first time we met each other was when Carlo got assigned to the Locale Congregation of De Castro, Metro Manila East. At that time, Johaira was with the other Light of Salvation Officers, doing an activity for the Christian Family Organization.

GETTING TO KNOW EACH OTHER

Serving together as church officers

Not long after, Carlo was assigned to be an overseer for the Light of Salvation Office in our Locale Congregation. From then on, we got to know each other more and more as we fulfilled our respective duties — attending different church activities together with our fellow officers, among them the 11th Anniversary Concert of Iglesia Ni Cristo at the Philippine Arena.

FALLING IN LOVE

Why God gave her to me

As I continued to know her deeply, I was amazed at how ready she was to sacrifice for the sake of her duties in the Church — giving so much of her time as a Light of Salvation Officer and choir member, while never neglecting her studies as an Information Technology student, and never failing to be a loving daughter to her family. Her unwavering love for the Church, her duties, her family, her faith, and her whole character — these, I believe, are the reasons God gave her to me.
